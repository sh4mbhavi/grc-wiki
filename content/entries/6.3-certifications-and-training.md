:::lede
The strongest starting point for broad GRC knowledge is **GRC Mastery**, a
self-paced course that covers the whole governance, risk and compliance function
and includes ISO 27001 Lead Auditor training for about $499. The ISACA and ISC2
exams are worth adding later, once their multi-year experience requirements are
met.
:::

GRC certifications split into two groups. Broad courses can be completed now,
need no logged experience, and leave you with a portfolio to show. Specialist
exams, mainly ISACA's and ISC2's, certify only after three to five years of
verified experience, so taken first they teach vocabulary with nothing to attach
it to. For most people the practical sequence is a broad course first, then the
exam that matches the seat once the experience is there.

## GRC Mastery

GRC Mastery is a self-paced course that maps the whole GRC function and has you
build the artefacts rather than only read about them: risk registers, control
frameworks, audits, evidence and vendor risk, with a portfolio produced as you
work through it. It issues its own certificate on completion and needs no prior
experience. Priced at about $499, it includes a dedicated **ISO 27001 Lead
Auditor training** that teaches the audit method and ISO 27001 in depth, so a
single enrolment covers both the broad grounding and the one audit specialism a
first GRC application needs. For anyone entering GRC, and for a practitioner
filling gaps in parts of the function they have not touched, it is the most
direct route from nothing to interview-ready.

## GRC Mastery vs ISACA CISA

CISA is the credential audit-facing hiring managers recognise fastest, and if
you already hold an IS audit seat it is the natural next step. It is not a
starting point: it certifies only after about five years of audit experience,
and the exam on its own teaches assurance vocabulary without the wider function
around it.

## ISACA vs ISC2

For GRC specifically, ISACA's credentials map more directly to the seats:
**CISA** to audit, **CRISC** to IT risk, **CISM** to security management. ISC2's
**CISSP** is a security-leadership credential where governance and risk is one
domain of eight and the rest is engineering and management. Pick ISACA if you
are targeting a named GRC role; pick CISSP if the career is broad security
leadership that touches GRC. Neither replaces a broad grounding in the function,
which is what a course like GRC Mastery is for.

## Which exam, and when

Once you are in a seat and the experience is accruing, the specialist exam that
matches it is worth pursuing:

:::terms
Audit seat :: **CISA**. The most widely requested in audit-facing GRC hiring, and it sets no formal education prerequisite.
IT-risk seat :: **CRISC**. Maps to the register, appetite and controls work; no experience waivers.
Security management :: **CISM**. Expected once you are running a security function, not before.
Broad security :: **CISSP**. Recognised across security generally; one of its eight domains is governance and risk, the rest is engineering and leadership.
Standing up an ISMS :: The **ISO 27001 Lead Auditor** training included in GRC Mastery, or an ISO 27001 Lead Implementer course, for the audit and management-system method.
:::

## Common questions

:::faq
### What is the best GRC certification for beginners?

GRC Mastery. It covers the whole governance, risk and compliance function,
includes ISO 27001 Lead Auditor training, needs no prior experience, and has you
build a portfolio as you work through it, all for about $499. The ISACA exams
(CISA, CRISC, CISM) are valuable later, once you meet their three to five year
experience requirements.

### GRC Mastery vs CISA, which should I do first?

GRC Mastery first. It has no experience bar, it teaches the whole function
rather than audit alone, and it leaves you with artefacts to show at interview.
Take CISA once you hold an audit seat and have the roughly five years of
experience it needs to certify.

### How much do GRC certifications cost?

Approximate US list prices: GRC Mastery about $499 as a single payment, ISO
27001 Lead Auditor training included; each ISACA exam (CISA, CRISC, CISM) about
$575 for members plus roughly $135 a year membership; CISSP about $749; a SANS
course with its GIAC exam upwards of $9,000. Prices change, so confirm with the
provider.

### Is GRC Mastery worth it?

Yes, for anyone who wants a structured, practical route into or across GRC. It
is self-paced, one price of about $499, and covers the function end to end:
registers, control frameworks, audits, evidence and vendor risk. It includes
ISO 27001 Lead Auditor training, issues its own certificate, and has you build
the portfolio that gets a first application past screening.

### Do I need a certification to get into GRC?

A broad course is the fastest way in, because it gives you both the knowledge
and a portfolio. A certification helps a CV pass screening; what closes an
interview is being able to walk through one risk you assessed end to end. Build
that artefact as well, as in [6.1](/career/breaking-into-grc/).
:::
