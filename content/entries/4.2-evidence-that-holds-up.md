:::lede
Evidence is a dated record produced by the operation of a control, sufficient
for a reviewer who was not present to conclude that the control ran as
described. Everything else is documentation.
:::

The gap between "we do this" and "we can show we did this" is where most first
audits are lost, and it is almost never because the control was not running.

## The four properties

:::terms
Dated :: The record shows when the control ran, in a way the system produced rather than a person typed. A screenshot with no visible timestamp establishes nothing about when.
Attributable :: It is clear who performed the action and who reviewed it. A shared account performing a control makes attribution impossible and usually makes the control itself a finding.
Complete for the period :: An auditor testing a quarterly control over a year expects four instances, not the best one. Evidence for three of four quarters is a gap, not a near miss.
Intelligible alone :: A stranger can tell from the artefact what happened. If it needs a colleague to narrate it, the auditor records it as unsupported.
:::

Completeness is the one that fails most often, and it fails quietly: a
programme collects excellent evidence for the months someone remembered.

## The screenshot problem

Screenshots are accepted evidence and are also the weakest common form. Cropped
to hide the URL, taken after the fact, showing a filtered view with the filter
out of frame, undated because the clock was outside the capture area.

Where a system can export, export. A CSV of the entitlement review with the
generation timestamp in it beats twelve screenshots, is faster to produce, and
cannot be accused of selection.

Where only a screenshot is possible, capture the whole window: URL bar, system
clock, the account signed in, and the filter state.

## What to keep, and for how long

Keep the artefact, the control it evidences, and the period it covers, linked so
that any one of the three retrieves the others. The retention floor is the audit
period plus one year, because the following year's audit will sample the prior
period to test consistency. Contracts and regulations frequently demand longer.

:::def term="Sampling" ref="4.3"
Testing a subset of a control's instances to conclude on the whole population.
The auditor selects the sample, which is why evidence must be complete rather
than representative.
:::

## The habit that fixes it

Collect at the point of operation, not at the point of request. A control that
files its own evidence (the review tool exporting on completion, the ticket
closing with the export attached) takes minutes once and survives audit. Evidence
gathered in the four weeks before fieldwork takes the whole four weeks and still
has holes in it.
