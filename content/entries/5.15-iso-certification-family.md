:::lede
ISO/IEC 27001 is one of a family of **management-system standards that share the
same skeleton** — context, leadership, planning, support, operation, evaluation,
improvement — so an organisation running one can add another without rebuilding
the machinery underneath it.
:::

That shared skeleton is called Annex SL, or the Harmonised Structure. It is why
clauses 4 to 10 look almost identical across ISO 27001, ISO 22301 and ISO 9001,
and why an internal audit programme, a management review and a corrective-action
process built for one can be extended to cover several. The differences are in
what each standard asks the management system to *do*.

Two shapes exist in the family. Some are standalone management systems you
certify against directly. Others are **extensions** or **sector code-of-practice**
documents that ride on top of an existing ISO 27001 certification.

## The standards a GRC function meets most

| Standard | What it manages | Shape | Certifiable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Information security | Standalone management system | Yes |
| ISO/IEC 27701 | Privacy (PII as controller or processor) | Extension to 27001 | Yes, as an extension |
| ISO/IEC 27017 | Security controls for cloud services | Code of practice on Annex A | Some bodies certify; often audited as an extension |
| ISO/IEC 27018 | Protection of PII in public cloud | Code of practice | As above |
| ISO 22301 | Business continuity | Standalone management system | Yes |
| ISO/IEC 20000-1 | IT service management | Standalone management system | Yes |
| ISO 9001 | Quality management | Standalone management system | Yes |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Artificial intelligence management | Standalone management system | Yes |

:::def term="Annex SL" ref="5.14"
The common high-level structure and identical core text that ISO requires all new
and revised management-system standards to follow, so that clauses 4–10 align and
multiple systems can be integrated into one.
:::

## How they stack

**ISO 27701** adds privacy-specific requirements and two extra control sets — one
for organisations acting as a data controller, one for processors — onto a 27001
ISMS. You cannot hold 27701 without 27001 (or certifying to both together). It is
the recognised way to show a structured response to GDPR and similar laws,
though it is not itself a legal compliance certificate.

**ISO 27017 and 27018** are code-of-practice documents for cloud. 27017 gives
cloud-specific implementation guidance and seven additional controls; 27018
focuses on personal data in public cloud. Cloud providers commonly hold all
three (27001, 27017, 27018) and customers ask for the set.

**ISO 22301** is a full, separate management system for business continuity —
covered at [3.6](/risk/business-continuity/) — with its own certification. It
shares the Annex SL structure with 27001, so the two integrate cleanly.

**ISO/IEC 42001**, published in 2023, is the first certifiable AI management
system standard. It asks an organisation to govern the AI it develops or uses —
impact assessment, data governance, transparency, human oversight — through the
same management-system pattern. It is becoming a procurement ask for AI vendors
the way 27001 did for cloud.

## Choosing what to add

Add a standard when a buyer, a regulator or a genuine risk asks for it, not to
collect certificates. Each additional certification is another audit, another
surveillance cycle and another set of records to maintain. The economical path is
one integrated management system with the scope widened to cover each standard,
audited together where the certification body allows it — not several parallel
systems with inconsistent risk registers.

## Common questions

:::faq
### What is the difference between ISO 27001 and ISO 27701?

ISO 27001 certifies an information security management system. ISO 27701 is a
privacy extension that adds data-protection requirements and controller- or
processor-specific controls on top of it. You need a 27001 ISMS to hold 27701;
27701 is the structured way to demonstrate privacy management, but it is not a
certificate of legal compliance with GDPR.

### What is ISO 42001?

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the international standard for an artificial intelligence
management system. It sets out how an organisation should govern its development
and use of AI — risk and impact assessment, data governance, transparency, human
oversight — using the same management-system structure as ISO 27001. It is
certifiable.

### Do ISO 27017 and 27018 replace ISO 27001?

No. They are codes of practice that add cloud-specific and cloud-privacy guidance
to an ISO 27001 ISMS. Cloud providers typically hold ISO 27001 together with
27017 and 27018, and customers ask for all three.

### Can multiple ISO management systems be combined?

Yes. Because they share the Annex SL structure, standards such as ISO 27001,
ISO 22301 and ISO 9001 can be run as one integrated management system with a
single internal audit programme, management review and corrective-action process,
and audited together. This is the standard approach for organisations holding
more than one.
:::
